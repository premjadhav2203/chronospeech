Title:
The Horizon of Human Curiosity

Transcript:
Consider for a moment why our ancestors looked across barren oceans and dared to build ships with neither map nor guarantee. They were compelled by an innate restlessness, an unrelenting desire to ask what lies beyond the curve of the horizon. Today, as we stand at the threshold of synthetic minds and deep space, that identical flame must guide our conscience. We cannot allow cynicism to blind our curiosity, nor complacency to dull our wonder. The future belongs to those who dare to question every frontier and venture beyond the edge.
