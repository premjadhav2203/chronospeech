Title:
Echoes of the Forgotten Valley

Transcript:
High above the river bends, where old pines lean against the northern wind, there lies a meadow untouched by time. The stones remember the footsteps of travelers who passed this way centuries ago, singing songs that the mountain mist swallowed whole. If you stand motionless at dusk, when the shadows stretch like reaching hands across the granite, the cold air carries a solitary hum. It is the voice of the earth, whispering that empires may rise and fade, but the mountain keeps its silent vigil through all human seasons.
