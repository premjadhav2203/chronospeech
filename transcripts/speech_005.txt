Title:
Bridges Over Cynicism

Transcript:
Cynicism is seductive because it costs nothing. It requires neither imagination nor vulnerability to declare that nothing will ever improve. But progress has never belonged to the spectator sitting comfortably in judgment. Progress belongs to the builders who pick up the hammer despite the heat, to the teachers who show up each day with patience, and to every citizen who chooses to build bridges where others build barricades. We must reject the comfort of despair and renew our faith in common purpose. A better world will not arrive uninvited; we must construct it together.
