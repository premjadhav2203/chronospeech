Title:
The Power of Small Decisions

Transcript:
We often wait for a monumental moment to redefine our lives, believing transformation arrives like thunder. But character is not forged in grand gestures; it is chiseled in the quiet, unremarkable choices we make every single morning. When you choose discipline over ease, when you choose patience over irritation, and when you choose honesty in the dark, you set an invisible trajectory in motion. Do not underestimate the quiet compounding of everyday conviction. In the end, greatness is never an accident of fate—it is the deliberate accumulation of small decisions.
