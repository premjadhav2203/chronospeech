Title:
Shadows and Lanterns: A Study in Perseverance

Transcript:
The night was thick and unforgiving, wrapping the damp road in a dense shroud of fog. Along the steep incline, a single traveler moved with measured steps, holding a solitary iron lantern whose flame flickered against the damp wind. Each gust threatened to extinguish the light, yet the hand remained steady and the journey continued unbroken. Darkness can appear infinite when viewed from afar, but darkness cannot advance upon a flame. One small lantern, carried with quiet persistence, is enough to pierce the longest shadows and illuminate the path ahead.
