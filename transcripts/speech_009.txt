Title:
The Duty of Empathy in Modern Times

Transcript:
In an age illuminated by glowing screens and separated by digital walls, human connection has paradoxically become fragile. We measure interactions in rapid clicks and fleeting reactions, mistaking visibility for understanding. But empathy is not an automatic instinct; it is an active moral discipline that demands our presence, our patience, and our willingness to listen without interrupting. When we look past the surface and truly acknowledge the burdens carried by our neighbors, we restore dignity to public life. Empathy is not a concession of weakness; it is the cornerstone of our shared survival.
