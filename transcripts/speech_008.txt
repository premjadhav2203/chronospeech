Title:
Navigating the Unknown City

Transcript:
Arriving in a foreign city at midnight forces an immediate shift in perspective. The street signs speak an unfamiliar tongue, the alleys twist in unforeseen directions, and all your conventional bearings dissolve in an instant. In that disorienting moment, you realize that adaptability is the greatest survival skill of modern life. When familiar landmarks vanish, you must rely on curiosity, intuition, and the kindness of strangers. Life rarely hands us a detailed itinerary; it invites us to explore the maze, embrace the detour, and discover strength in unexpected places.
