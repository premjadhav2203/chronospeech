Title:
Courage in the Crucible of Uncertainty

Transcript:
History has never been shaped by those who waited for all risk to dissolve before taking their first step. Every revolution of thought, every voyage into the uncharted wilderness, began in the heavy fog of the unknown. When doubt surrounds you, remember that certainty is an illusion of the timid. Courage does not require the absence of fear; it demands the conviction that the purpose ahead matters far more than the comfort left behind. Stand resolute, meet the storm with unwavering focus, and let your actions declare who you are.
