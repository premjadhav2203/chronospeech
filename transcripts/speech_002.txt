Title:
The Architecture of Silence

Transcript:
Listen closely to the room when the clock stops ticking. Silence is rarely empty; it is a canvas waiting for thought. In our noisy cities, we rush to fill every pause with chatter, fearful of what the stillness might reveal. Yet the greatest symphonies draw their power not from the barrage of brass, but from the breath held between the notes. When we learn to inhabit stillness without anxiety, we discover a sanctuary where insight can finally breathe, and where our restless intentions finally align with our deepest truths.
