Title:
The Discipline of the Open Mind

Transcript:
It is effortless to defend beliefs that confirm our pride, and effortless to dismiss perspectives that disrupt our peace. But intellect without humility quickly decays into dogmatism. To genuinely learn, one must cultivate the courage to be proven wrong. We must welcome rigorous debate, scrutinize our own assumptions with unyielding honesty, and hold truth higher than tribal allegiance. The measure of wisdom is not how fiercely you cling to old dogma, but how gracefully your mind expands when confronted with evidence. Commit to understanding before you rush to condemn.
